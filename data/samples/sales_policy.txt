Discount Policy

Sales representatives may grant discounts of up to 10% without approval. Discounts above 10% require written approval from the regional sales manager.

Returns Policy

Electronics may be returned within 30 days of delivery. Furniture returns are accepted within 14 days and incur a restocking fee of 15%.

Regional Targets

The North region target for 2025 is 15% revenue growth. The West region is expected to expand into two new markets during the second half of the year.
